Pet Food Bank

Free food for pet owners who need it. All leftover donations go directly to local shelters.

We are very low on donations

You may need to wait a few days or weeks before we can schedule a pick-up appointment. We have many requests and limited volunteer capacity. Please call us if you are in urgent need of free pet food.

Need food?

How to get help

Call us and we'll set up a time for you to pick up free pet food. We serve all kinds of pets and appreciate your patience as we do our best with available donations.

Call 415-564-6482
Want to donate?

What we accept

  • Pet food — opened or recently expired is fine
  • Clean towels — used for cleaning, extras go to shelters
  • Fresh vegetables and fruit for small mammals, reptiles, and birds (romaine, leaf lettuce, chard, bananas, bell peppers)
  • Used tanks, cages, and enclosures
  • Substrate, decor, food bowls, and water bottles

Please disinfect cage donations before drop-off when possible.
