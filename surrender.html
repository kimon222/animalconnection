Who we accept

Pets we welcome

We accept (but are not limited to): guinea pigs, rats, hamsters, parakeets, budgies, cockatiels, conures, tortoises, leopard geckos, bearded dragons, corn snakes, milksnakes, kingsnakes, crested geckos, veiled chameleons, jackson's chameleons, hermit crabs, tropical freshwater schooling fish, whites tree frogs, firebelly toads, and more.

We do not accept: dogs, cats, boas/pythons, spiders, or rabbits.

Current Waiting List

We currently have no space for guinea pigs or bearded dragons. We have a waiting list — please note it may take several weeks to months for your name to come up. Email info@animalconnectionsf.net to join.
