Who we are

SF's largest
independent pet store

Since 1987 we have been the city's go-to for expert animal advice, premium pet diets, and genuine care. Our staff doesn't just work with animals — they live with them, advocate for them, and know them.

We do not sell dogs, cats, or rabbits and always suggest adoption. We do not support breeding mills. We work closely with rescue organisations and responsible local breeders to save as many animals as we can.

Come in and feel the difference a truly independent, animal-first store makes.

Featured programme

Handfed

Baby Budgie Programme

The Budgerigar — "budgie" — is a small parrot from Australia with one of the largest speech capabilities of any bird its size. They make great companions for all kinds of people, including children. After we finish hand-feeding them, they're sweet, playful, curious birds that genuinely bond with their humans.

They're one of the only birds we recommend for kids — and one of our greatest joys.

We accept donations

Greens for
small mammals

Donate fresh produce and we'll feed our rescues. Leftovers go to local shelters. Here's what helps — and what doesn't.

✓  Acceptable produce
  • Romaine, Red & Green leaf lettuce
  • Dandelion Greens
  • Carrot Tops
  • Chard (swiss or rainbow)
  • Wheatgrass
  • Mustard Greens
✕  Please avoid
  • Iceberg lettuce
  • Cucumber
  • Tomato
  • Celery
  • Broccoli
  • Avocado
  • Potato

We also accept pet food (opened or recently expired), clean towels, used tanks and cages, substrate, decor, and food bowls — all go to animals in need.
