Animal Boarding

We pride ourselves on the way we care for your animals. You won't find a more qualified, more compassionate group of experts to watch your pets while you're away.

Who we welcome

Animals we board

🐦

Birds

All birds — canaries, budgies, conures, parrots, finches, and more.

🐹

Small Mammals

Guinea pigs, rabbits, hamsters, chinchillas, rats, and mice.

🦎

Reptiles & Amphibians

All reptiles and amphibians permitted in San Francisco. (No boas, pythons, front/rear-fanged or venomous animals per SF municipal code.)

Standards of care

What you can expect

  • Fresh food and water provided every day
  • Animals cleaned and checked on throughout the day
  • Animals handled only by Animal Connection staff (or vet if necessary)
Complimentary house diet
Small Mammals

Oxbow pellets, timothy hay, and fresh greens (typically romaine, dandelion greens, and other dark leafy greens)

Reptiles & Amphibians

Variety of bugs (crickets, mealworms, dubia roaches, waxworms) and fresh greens depending on diet requirements

Birds

Species-appropriate seeds and occasional treats

Fees

Boarding rates

Per Night
Own cage $15 / day
Rental cage $20 / day

* All boarders must arrive in appropriately sized, clean cages. Please bring a sizable cage — it will be your pet's only space for their stay.

Before you drop off
  • Cage must be clean and in good condition
  • Bring the lid — we do not accept open-top cages or tanks
  • Bring your animal's specific diet and any treats
  • Notify us of any special medical instructions ahead of time
  • Make a reservation — we fill up during holidays and summers
