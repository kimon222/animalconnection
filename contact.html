Donations welcome

We take donations for all pets. Pet food is used for our pet food bank — where we give free food to pet parents. All extra food goes to local shelters. We accept food that is opened or recently expired.

Clean towels are used for cleaning up after animals. Any unused towels go to local shelters.

We accept fresh veggies and fruit for our small mammals, reptiles, and birds — favourites include romaine, green and red leaf lettuce, chard, bananas, and bell peppers.

We also accept used tanks, cages, enclosures, substrate, decor, and food bowls/water bottles. Please try to disinfect donations before drop-off.
